Pin a demo window from the hourly table
Tara picks a concrete demo window straight off a day's hourly rows and pins it to the top, then watches its go/no-go status firm up each morning as the date approaches. The demo length is fixed (set in Window Controls), so picking a window is just choosing where it sits: point at the middle of the stretch you want and the fixed-length block centers on it, then click to confirm and pin.
