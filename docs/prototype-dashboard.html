Prototype — runs on illustrative mock data so every status is visible. In the build, the two hourly arrays come straight from the Open-Meteo Forecast + Marine endpoints (no key, browser-direct). The horizon line is the spine: each day's daylight hours (07:00–19:00), colored by Tara's thresholds, with the best contiguous 6-hour demo window highlighted. A day's date is colored by its best available window — so a day still counts as a candidate if any 6-hour block holds up, even when the rest of the day is out of bounds. Tap any day to load its full read above, trim the window, and pin it.
